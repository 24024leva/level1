Over and underfeeding

One of the problems many pet owners face is their pets being overfed or underfed, which may impact the animal’s health. Overfeeding can result in obesity, weight gain, joint pains, and diseases. On the other hand, underfeeding may contribute to weight loss, weakness, fatigue, and nutrient deficiency.

Exercise

How to know if your pet is getting enough excersise

Excersise your pet

A lack of exercise in pets can lead to many problems including weight gain, low energy, boredom and behavioral problems. If you suspect that your pet is not getting enough exercise then look out for signs of restlessness, destructive behavior, weight gain and of lots of energy that is not being used.
